# Visual Concept Worker Mechanics Ringer

This fast public-safe ringer qualifies two framework mechanics independently of
model inference and private benchmark content.

The Open Images lane verifies explicit human-present/human-absent labels, keeps
unannotated concepts unknown, accepts verification-style human provenance, and
fails closed on machine-source rows or missing class descriptions.

The active-controller lane verifies declared action budgets and toolsets, valid
parent-observation references, controller-owned deterministic observation IDs,
policy-stop versus budget-exhausted termination, and reproducible execution
digests. All perception output is synthetic.

The ringer makes no model-performance or policy-quality claim. It exists to
qualify the evidence and orchestration invariants cheaply before those mechanics
are connected to real models or private benchmark assets.
